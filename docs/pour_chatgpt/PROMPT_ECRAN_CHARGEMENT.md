Using the attached character sheet as the exact reference for the characters (same designs, colors,
outfits and drawing style: chibi proportions, clean dark outlines, soft cel shading), paint a splash
illustration for the loading screen of a mobile fantasy RPG called CREOS.

Format: landscape 1920x1080, full illustration, no text, no logo, no UI.

Scene: an epic group shot of a band of hero hunters at dusk on the docks of a fantasy harbor town,
lanterns glowing, ships and a crystal tower in the background, magic sparks in the air.
Put 6 heroes from the sheet in dynamic battle-ready poses, overlapping, at different depths:
Aegis (knight with sun shield) in the center front, Corbin (plague-mask rogue) leaping on the left,
Mei Lian (swordswoman) and Saffran (fox) on the right, Brume (hooded mage with glowing staff) and
Kharok (beetle warrior) behind.

Composition: keep the top 30% fairly calm (a game logo will be placed there) and the bottom 15% darker
and plain (the loading bar will go there). Strong warm/cool contrast, readable on a phone.
